The npm lockfile and the desktop/helper go.mod and go.sum files record this revision’s dependencies. Desktop packages use the operating system WebView (WKWebView, WebView2 or WebKitGTK); they do not bundle Electron or Chromium. Before production distribution, inventory the exact linked and bundled dependencies and supply their license/source notices. Development-only tools are not bundled.

The unmodified Xray-core executable is obtained from https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core/releases/tag/v26.10.31. Corresponding source is available at https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core/tree/v26.10.31 (source archive: https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core/archive/refs/tags/v26.10.31.tar.gz). No Xray source modifications were made. Exact upstream archive and executable SHA-256 values are recorded in resources/core-lock.json. The bundled executable must retain its LICENSE and this source-availability notice. The binary geosite/geoip files are not bundled; the core receives explicit rule lists converted from them.

Rule data: resources/rules/baseline.json is generated by tools/import-rules.py from the v2fly releases pinned in resources/rules/manifest.json (https://github.com/v2fly/domain-list-community, MIT; https://github.com/v2fly/geoip, CC-BY-SA-4.0). The conversion selects categories, deduplicates domains covered by earlier sections and collapses address ranges; the adapted rule data is shared under CC-BY-SA-4.0 where it derives from v2fly/geoip. Remote rule updates are produced the same way from the then-current releases. All rule data is provided without warranty.
